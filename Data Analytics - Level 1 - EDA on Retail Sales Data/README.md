# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Objective

The objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to uncover sales patterns, customer behaviour trends, product performance, and actionable business insights.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset

The dataset contains retail transaction information including:

- Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount

## Data Analysis Performed

The following analyses were performed in this project:

- Dataset structure and initial inspection
- Data type and data quality checks
- Descriptive statistical analysis
- Monthly and quarterly sales trend analysis
- Customer age-group analysis
- Gender-wise customer analysis
- Product category analysis
- Revenue analysis by product category
- Quantity analysis
- Correlation analysis using a heatmap
- Quantity vs. Total Amount analysis
- Customer spending behaviour analysis
- High-value transaction analysis
- Additional visualizations to identify business insights

## Key Insights

The analysis provides insights into:

- Changes in sales performance over time
- Customer participation and spending behaviour across demographic groups
- Differences in revenue and sales volume between product categories
- Relationships between numerical variables such as quantity, price per unit, age, and total amount
- High-value transactions and their contribution to overall sales

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings from the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

1. **Focus on High-Revenue Product Categories**  
   Prioritize inventory and promotional activities for product categories generating higher revenue.

2. **Target High-Value Customer Segments**  
   Use personalized promotions, loyalty programs, and premium product recommendations for customer groups with higher spending.

3. **Plan Inventory Around Sales Trends**  
   Use monthly and quarterly sales patterns to improve inventory planning and prepare stock before high-demand periods.

4. **Promote Lower-Performing Categories**  
   Use targeted discounts, product bundles, and promotional campaigns to improve lower-performing product categories.

5. **Encourage Higher Transaction Values**  
   Use cross-selling and product-bundling strategies to encourage customers to purchase more products per transaction.

## Conclusion

The exploratory data analysis provides a better understanding of retail sales patterns, customer behaviour, product category performance, and relationships between numerical variables.

The insights obtained from the analysis can support data-driven decisions related to inventory planning, customer targeting, product promotion, and improving overall sales performance.
